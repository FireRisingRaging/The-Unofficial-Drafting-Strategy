      Art of The Draft

      The World Needs One More Architectural Digest

      To both new and seasoned architects, the name Drafting Strategy is synonymous to a bible, a book preaching the gospel of good drafting practices. Yet with the Drafting Studio Press going dark and what I see was too many architects running afowl, I take it to my own flippers to collate important advices that has not yet been covered by the previous five volumes.

      In this extra-long issue of "UNOFFICIAL DRAFTING STRATEGY", I will bring a collection of important concepts, guidelines and examples to test your understanding against the veterans of drafting.
